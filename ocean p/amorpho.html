Amorphous Data provides an AI-powered subject matter expert that shares answers based on internal company knowledge.

Their solution is fully deployed on DigitalOcean’s Kubernetes infrastructure, utilizing a large Kubernetes cluster with GPUs for AI training, inference, and personality customization. They also leverage DigitalOcean’s Gradient GPU-optimized nodes for cost-effective workload management, running different containers on GPU or CPU nodes as needed. DigitalOcean’s Kubernetes and GPU solutions offer Amorphous Data a scalable, reliable, and cost-efficient infrastructure, ensuring seamless deployment and management. Tune in to learn more!
